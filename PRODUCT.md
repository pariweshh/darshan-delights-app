# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Australian shoppers (primarily South Asian, Nepalese, and Indian diaspora communities) buying specialty groceries, snacks, spices, and sweets. They shop on mobile during commutes, lunch breaks, or while planning meals. They know what they want but also browse for discoveries. Many are repeat buyers who reorder staples.

## Product Purpose

Darshan Delights is a mobile e-commerce app for a specialty South Asian grocery store in Australia. It lets users browse curated product categories, discover new items, manage a cart, and check out with Stripe (including Apple Pay). The app also supports push notifications for order updates and promotions.

Success means: repeat purchases, high cart conversion, and a shopping experience that feels culturally connected rather than generic.

## Brand Personality

Warm, Authentic, Vibrant.

The brand should feel like walking into a well-stocked, lively specialty grocery store, not a sterile supermarket aisle. Colors are rich, imagery is appetizing, and the experience respects the user's cultural knowledge without over-explaining. The tone is friendly and confident, never clinical or corporate.

## Anti-references

- **Generic grocery apps** (Woolworths, Coles, Instacart): cold, utilitarian, blue/white corporate palette, zero personality.
- **Typical ethnic food apps**: cramped layouts, low-quality imagery, cluttered navigation, no visual hierarchy, feels like a WordPress plugin.

## Design Principles

1. **Appetizing first**: product images and food photography drive engagement. Every screen should make the user hungry.
2. **Cultural warmth over corporate polish**: use color, typography, and imagery that feel rooted in South Asian vibrancy, not Silicon Valley minimalism.
3. **Mobile-native density**: information density matters on small screens. Don't waste space on decorative padding; prioritize product visibility.
4. **Trust through clarity**: pricing, shipping, and checkout must be transparent and frictionless. No dark patterns.
5. **Respect the repeat buyer**: favorites, reorder flows, and cart persistence matter more than onboarding wizards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Standard mobile accessibility (VoiceOver/TalkBack support)
- Text must remain readable at system font scale up to 1.5x
- Touch targets minimum 44pt
- Color contrast: WCAG AA minimum for all text
